# Getting help

| Channel | Who it is for | What it is for | Where | Public? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Community | Everyone | Questions, ideas | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/voplica/help/discussions) | Yes |
| Direct support | Every account — Base and Premium for any question; without a plan for billing, legal and DMCA notices, security vulnerabilities and bugs (up to three open) | Your account, a run, a notice, a vulnerability, a bug | The [Support page](https://voplica.com/support) in the studio — the fastest route | No, private |
| Bug reports | Everyone | Something does not work as it should | The studio's support form (fastest, private), or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/voplica/help/issues) (public, may be slower) | Depends |
| Legal and DMCA | Everyone, with or without an account | Legal questions, DMCA notices and counter-notices | A Legal and DMCA notices issue in the studio (fastest); or legal@voplica.com with [DMCA.md](DMCA.md) completed and signed | No, private |
| Security | Everyone | Security vulnerabilities | A Security vulnerability issue in the studio (fastest); or the private routes in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) | No, private |

## What to include

- What you did, what you expected and what happened.
- Where: which page of the studio, the API, or Voplica in Claude or ChatGPT (the MCP connector).
- Your browser and device.
- Your own run ID, if a run is involved: the run cards in your generation history show it.

## What never to post in public

- Passwords, API keys (they start with `pat_`) or any other token.
- Payment details.
- Anyone else's personal data or run IDs.
- Explicit images or links to explicit material.
