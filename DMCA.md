# Copyright (DMCA) notices

Voplica responds to notices of claimed copyright infringement under 17 U.S.C. §512 (the DMCA).

**The fastest route is the studio.** Sign in to [voplica.com](https://voplica.com), open the [Support page](https://voplica.com/support) and open a new issue in the category **Legal and DMCA notices**. Every account can, free plans included. Paste the completed form below as the description and type your full name as your signature. The issue keeps the whole conversation in one private place and tells you when we answer.

**Email works too, but only with this form completed and signed**: send it to legal@voplica.com. Email is less visible to us, receives spam, and is answered more slowly than an issue in the studio.

## The notice form

Copy everything between the lines, fill in every item, and send it as described above. A notice that misses an item is answered with what is missing, not acted on.

---

**1. The copyrighted work** that you say is infringed (a title, a description, a link to the original — or a representative list if there are many):

**2. The material you want removed**, and where it is on Voplica — the link to each item, or its ID, so that we can find it:

**3. Your contact information**: full name; postal address; telephone number; email address:

**4. Good-faith statement**: I have a good-faith belief that the use of the material described above is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

**5. Accuracy statement**: I swear, under penalty of perjury, that the information in this notice is accurate and that I am the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed, or am authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

**6. Signature**: your full name, typed (an electronic signature), and the date:

## Counter-notice

If material you uploaded was removed or disabled after a notice and you believe that was a mistake or a misidentification, send a counter-notice the same way (a Legal and DMCA notices issue in the studio, or legal@voplica.com) with:

1. the material that was removed and where it appeared before it was removed (its link or ID);
2. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification;
3. your full name, postal address and telephone number;
4. a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or, if outside the United States, any judicial district in which Voplica may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the notice or their agent;
5. your full name, typed (an electronic signature), and the date.

## What happens next

A complete notice leads to the material being removed or disabled, and the uploader being told. A complete counter-notice is forwarded to the person who sent the notice; the material may be restored no sooner than 10 and no later than 14 business days after we receive the counter-notice, unless that person tells us they have filed a court action. Accounts that repeatedly infringe are terminated.
