# Content and safety

How Voplica keeps generations within its content rules, and what to do when a request is blocked or an item is flagged.

## How does Voplica keep the platform safe?

Our own safety systems check prompts, uploaded and referenced images and videos, and generated results. For prompts and references we also use third-party safety services, the OpenAI Moderation API and TypeSafe's JEV, as part of that check. Flagged items are reviewed by our moderators.

## How old do I have to be to use Voplica?

You must be at least 18 years old to use Voplica. By signing up for or signing in to Voplica you confirm that you are at least 18 years old.

## Why was my generation blocked?

Our safety systems flagged the request. A generation stopped before it runs is not charged. We don't share the details of a decision, so that the systems stay effective.

## Why did a third-party model refuse my request?

Some models are run by other companies under their own safety rules. If their systems stop a generation, your credits are returned.

## Why can't I publish an item?

Public items must meet our public content rules, and our ratings can be stricter than you might expect. Your private items stay private.

## What happens when an item is flagged?

Our safety systems can flag an item, including a private one, and a moderator reviews it. The item may be unavailable until the moderator decides. There are three outcomes:

- **The flag was a mistake.** The item is approved and you can use it again. Your account is not affected.
- **The model produced it without being asked.** The item is taken down. Your account is not affected.
- **A moderator confirms that you meant to create it.** The item is taken down and your account's standing is reduced.

Repeated violations close the account, and the person may not register again.

## How do I report content?

Sign in, open the item, voice, model, character, dataset, workflow or profile and choose **Report**: it is in the ⋯ menu or behind the flag icon. Pick the reasons and add details if you can; our team will review your report. A report is not a legal notice: for a copyright notice, use the form in [DMCA.md](DMCA.md). This forum is public, so never post the reported content here.

## Can I appeal a decision?

Open a support issue from the [Support page](https://voplica.com/support); a moderator will look at it. Choose the category **Content moderation**. [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) says who can open which issues.

## Private by design

Your private items are visible only to you — and to our moderators when our safety systems flag one for review. Choose the Sensitive mode to keep a job's GPU work off public marketplace hardware.
